<pb> – Page beginning / page break milestone (e.g., <pb n="1" facs="#Front_page"/>)

  
<p> – Paragraph block

  
<lb> – Line beginning / line break milestone (e.g., <lb n="1"/>)

  
<date> – Date expression (e.g., <date when="1791-12-31">)

  
<choice> – Groups alternative encodings for a single word or token

  
<abbr> – Abbreviated form of a word or phrase (e.g., MM)

  
<expan> – Expansion of an abbreviation (e.g., Messieurs)

  
<orig> – Original orthographic form as found in the source text

  
<reg> – Regularized / normalized standard modern form
